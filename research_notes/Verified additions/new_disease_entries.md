# Verification notes for the disease entries added in October 2026

Entries: tomato bacterial wilt and Septoria leaf spot, rice brown spot and sheath blight,
maize common rust and gray leaf spot, wheat brown rust and aphid, onion basal rot.

Each practice was checked against ICAR / state-university extension material or published
Indian field trials. Practices with no supporting source were removed (see "Dropped").
Doses that could not be confirmed are left to the product label rather than invented.
**Have a KVK / HPKV agronomist review before presenting as locally validated advice.**

## Tomato bacterial wilt (Ralstonia solanacearum)
- Rotation with non-host crops, disease-free seedlings, limited irrigation, neem cake 100 kg/acre,
  bleaching-powder drench 4 kg/acre: TNAU Agritech, https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/crop_protection/tomato_diseases_5.html
- Lime, bleaching powder and oil cakes (karanj cake best for survival, 69.7%; lime largest yield gain),
  applied 15 days before transplanting: ICAR trials, https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAgS/article/view/27858
  and https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAgS/article/view/59855
- Disease worse in acidic soil (pH < 6.0) and saturated soil at 30-35 C: krishikosh epidemiology thesis,
  https://krishikosh.egranth.ac.in/items/cfbffde9-e8f0-4108-b227-2fd3a6e66eae
- Grafting on Solanum torvum: Indian Phytopathology 69(3):260-265, https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IPPJ/article/view/60167
  (tomato grafts showed 23-40% wilt in a coastal-India study; ungrafted controls 93-100%).
- Water-glass (ooze) test: standard diagnostic, not from the sources above.
- Bleaching powder is a chemical disinfectant, so it is not shown as a card in this natural-farming app.

## Tomato Septoria leaf spot
- HPKV Palampur trials: wider spacing, staking with lower-leaf removal, and maize intercropping lowered severity:
  https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IPPJ/article/view/19208
- 2-4 inch organic mulch as a splash barrier, 3-4 year rotation, base watering, removing nightshade/horsenettle weeds,
  clearing and tilling debris: Iowa State, https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/septoria-leaf-spot
- Foliar Trichoderma viride / T. harzianum reduced severity (less than mancozeb) in a J&K hills trial:
  https://gnanaganga.inflibnet.ac.in:8443/jspui/bitstream/123456789/11145/1/Yield%20assessment%20and%20evaluation%20of%20different%20fungicides%20and%20bio%20agents%20on%20the%20foliar%20diseases%20of%20tamoto%20under%20field%20conditions%20in%20Hills.pdf

## Rice brown spot
- Hot-water seed treatment 53-54 C for 10-12 min (8 h cold pre-soak helps), Pseudomonas fluorescens 10 g/kg seed,
  seedling dip, disease-free seed, proper nutrition, avoid water stress, silicon where deficient:
  TNAU, https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/expert_system/paddy/cpdisbrownspot.html

## Rice sheath blight
- P. fluorescens seed treatment 10 g/kg + 0.2% foliar sprays at 30 and 45 days after transplanting + neem cake
  250 kg/ha gave an 81% reduction over control: Kannan et al., J. Pharmacognosy & Phytochemistry 2019,
  https://phytojournal.com/archives/2019/vol8issue2S/PartQ/SP-8-2-214-929.pdf
- Neem cake + Trichoderma harzianum cut incidence by up to 57% in basmati rice: J. Biological Control,
  https://informaticsjournals.com/index.php/jbc/article/view/3679
- Spacing, nitrogen splitting, drainage and weed removal are standard extension guidance (not individually sourced here).

## Maize common rust
- Resistant hybrids are the main control (Dharwad screening, 14 of 43 hybrids resistant); sanitation, rotation,
  early-maturing varieties: https://www.ojs.pphouse.org/index.php/IJBSM/article/view/1151 and
  https://krishijagran.com/featured/major-diseases-of-maize-and-their-management/

## Maize gray leaf spot
- Overwinters on residue, worse after maize and in reduced tillage; burying residue, rotation, resistant hybrids,
  favoured by 20-30 C and long humid spells: https://cropwatch.unl.edu/plant-disease/corn/gray-leaf-spot/ and
  https://pps.agriculturejournals.cz/pdfs/pps/2013/01/03.pdf. Maize-bean intercrop gave 21% higher yield in an African trial.

## Wheat brown rust
- Resistant varieties with diverse resistance are the economic control; new pathotypes keep emerging in HP
  (Flowerdale, Shimla): https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAgS/article/view/55211
- Removing volunteer wheat and avoiding late heavy nitrogen are standard guidance (not individually sourced here).

## Wheat aphid
- Aqueous neem extract 74% and NSKE 10% 69% reduction after 7 days versus 94% for thiamethoxam, with more
  ladybirds in neem plots: https://indianentomology.org/index.php/ije/article/view/134
- NSKE 5% harmless to natural enemies; yellow sticky traps catch winged aphids at peak: Pantnagar,
  https://krishikosh.egranth.ac.in/items/890e14ae-b336-4c38-bf83-db03b0376d1d

## Onion basal rot
- 250-gauge polythene solarization of moist soil for 30 days in summer, 4-5 year rotation, drainage, deep summer
  ploughing, avoid injury, flooding in the off season: https://celkau.in/crops/vegetables/onion/basalrot.php and
  NHB onion guide, https://www.nhb.gov.in/pdf/vegetable/onion/oni002.pdf
- Trichoderma-mixed compost (500 kg/ha) gave the highest yield in a BARI field study:
  https://www.banglajol.info/index.php/BJAR/article/view/91932/59167

## Dropped because no supporting evidence was found
- Sour buttermilk spray and cow-urine spray (all rust / Septoria entries). Only raw milk/whey sprays have small
  powdery-mildew studies; no trial of fermented buttermilk or cow urine against these diseases was found.
- Neem seed kernel spray for rice brown spot, neem leaf extract for gray leaf spot, neem cake for basal rot,
  Trichoderma compost for gray leaf spot, mustard/cabbage residue biofumigation for Ralstonia, soap-water for aphid.
- Unverified exact quantities (Trichoderma/Pseudomonas per 100 kg FYM, 250 g neem cake per pit, 10 traps/acre).
